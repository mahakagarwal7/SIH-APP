## Behavior and scope

Roadmap 1.6 completes submitted-report status updates on native My reports. It reads each visible report’s production extraction job and current claims, then keeps processing, retry/failure, planner review, clarification, verification and final outcomes distinct. The initial 5-second foreground check backs off to at most 60 seconds and stops once all visible submitted reports are terminal.

Polling pauses offline, while the app is inactive, and when My reports loses focus or unmounts. Refresh errors retain the last known result with an explicit warning. This PR uses polling because production Realtime publication has not been verified.

This PR is stacked on **PR #8, `feature/1.4-confirmation-submit`**. It changes no production schema, RLS policy, RPC, web route or worker.

## Contract and decisions

- Reads the signed-in author’s existing `reports`, `jobs` and `claims` rows through the typed Supabase client and production RLS. A current-version extraction job takes precedence over any earlier claim outcome.
- Mirrors the web status precedence, including processing failure, retry wait, Needs review, Answer needed, Supervisor check, disputed evidence, Accepted, Partly accepted, Rejected, Observed, Unplanned, mixed final outcomes and Withdrawn.
- Rejects malformed or foreign claim/job report IDs rather than rendering a partial mixed-author response.
- Treats refresh time as a check time only; it does not invent a server update timestamp.
- Makes no background-while-closed or Realtime claim.

See [the submitted status contract](../REPORT_STATUS.md), [outbox contract](../OUTBOX_SYNC.md) and [approved delivery plan](../DELIVERY_PLAN.md).

## Manual device and production checks

1. Supply the publishable key locally, sign in as the designated reporter and submit a synthetic report.
2. Open My reports and confirm the sequence shows Processing report before Needs review; it must not say Awaiting review or Accepted before production creates the corresponding outcome.
3. Exercise a worker retry and terminal failure. Confirm Waiting to retry and Processing needs attention remain distinct and internal error codes are not presented as user instructions.
4. Move one report through pending, clarification, verification, accepted/rejected, observed and unplanned outcomes on the planner web app. Confirm the native status refreshes with the matching distinction.
5. Background the app for longer than the polling interval and verify no requests occur. Resume and verify status checking restarts.
6. Disable connectivity. Confirm the offline banner preserves the last loaded state without claiming a new check; reconnect and verify refreshing resumes.
7. Switch accounts and confirm cached report/job/claim rows from the first account are unavailable.
8. Leave My reports and verify its timer stops; return and verify a fresh status read.
