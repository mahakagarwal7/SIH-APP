## Behavior and scope

Roadmap 1.3 adds native typed reports and up to three captioned photos. Text and
normalized JPEG bytes are copied into account-scoped SQLite/app-private storage
before the screen says **Saved on device**. Failed saves retain the same draft
identity for retry. Drafts survive logout, remain visible only to their owner
and require explicit discard.

Report now offers Voice / Type / Photo routes. Type and Photo share one draft
form so evidence can include both wording and photos. Camera/library denial,
empty drafts, missing project context, offline state, incomplete storage and
Android picker-result recovery are explicit. Web shows the native requirement.

This PR is stacked on **PR #5, `feature/1.2-voice-capture`**. No upload,
transcription, confirmation, submission, backend mutation or PR merge is part
of this slice.

## Contract and assumptions

- Existing production media boundaries: at most three photos, JPEG/PNG/WebP,
  at most 5 MiB each and at most 20 million decoded pixels. Mobile deliberately
  normalizes all picker inputs to actual JPEG bytes.
- Text is at most 10,000 characters; photo captions are at most 500 characters.
- `expo-image-picker` handles camera/library selection;
  `expo-image-manipulator` performs bounded decode/resize/JPEG conversion.
- The owner-approved SQLite/app-private, retain-on-logout, same-account and
  explicit-discard rules now cover text/photo drafts.
- The approved delivery plan puts outbox 1.5 before confirmation 1.4 because
  voice transcripts exist only after upload and media processing.

See [the persistence and photo contract](../TEXT_PHOTO_CAPTURE.md) and the
[approved delivery sequence](../DELIVERY_PLAN.md).

## Manual device checks

1. Build/install the native app. Sign in as an authorized field account and
   open Report → Type. Enter work completed and remaining, save and restart.
   Confirm the draft remains **Saved on device · Not sent for review**.
2. Open Report → Photo. Deny camera access and confirm an honest error without
   a draft. Grant access, take a photo, add a caption and save. Repeat from the
   library, including HEIC or another non-JPEG input where available.
3. Enable Android “Don't keep activities,” choose/take a photo and return.
   Confirm the pending result is recovered once, normalized and remains ready
   to save.
4. Add three photos. Confirm both add actions disable at the limit. Remove one,
   add another, and verify captions remain attached to the intended images.
5. Enable airplane mode after project context has loaded. Save text/photo,
   restart with a valid session and verify the local draft remains. Nothing
   should appear in the planner web app.
6. Log out, sign into another account and confirm the first account's drafts are
   absent. Sign back in, cancel discard once, then confirm discard and restart;
   metadata and private photos must be gone.
7. Exercise insufficient storage/interrupted save. The screen must not claim
   success; Retry save must reuse the same draft content and identifier.
