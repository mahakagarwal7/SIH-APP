# PR: Complete the field-report regression matrix

## Behavior and scope

FIXES step 7.5 runs the complete Feature 2 report matrix through the durable
outbox and My Reports model. Voice (V), text (T), photo (P), VT, VP, TP and VTP
all reserve, upload applicable media, finalize, inspect, confirm and submit in
the required order. Every combination is checked while saved locally, awaiting
review and accepted.

My Reports now names the evidence actually retained for the report: **Voice**,
**Text**, **Photo**, or the applicable combined label. This prevents typed text
or supporting photos from disappearing behind the former generic **Voice
report** label. Attachment counts remain separate and remote-only reports still
say **Production evidence** when their attachment composition was not queried.

The regression found one misleading summary: a voice-only capture awaiting its
transcript could display **0 photos submitted as evidence**. It now displays
**Voice transcription pending.** Partial wording remains verbatim, including
remaining quantities and inspection caveats, without conversion to a percent.

The durable outbox phase retains the blue **Uploading** status added in FIXES

delivery still uses the **Saved on device** status. This keeps saved and sent
states distinct while accurately showing active background work.

## Contract and decisions

- Production tables, RPC names, payloads and RLS behavior are unchanged.
- The matrix asserts `reserve_field_capture` before uploads,
  `finalize_field_media` after every applicable upload, media inspection after
  finalize, and `submit_field_capture` only after confirmation.
- Text-only reports reserve and submit without inventing a media upload or
  finalize call.
- My Reports continues to deduplicate local, outbox and production rows by the
  immutable capture identity.
- Remote-only evidence composition remains unknown rather than inferred from
  `source_kind`.

## Manual production checks

Use a permitted test project and disposable field reports. For each of V, T,
P, VT, VP, TP and VTP:

1. Capture the named evidence while offline. Open My Reports and verify the row
   says **Saved on device** or **Uploading** with the explicit saved-on-device
   detail, shows the exact evidence label and never claims it was sent.
2. Reconnect and sync. Verify applicable media uploads in the background, the
   confirmed report reaches **Awaiting review**, and its wording, file count and
   evidence label remain correct.
3. Accept every extracted claim from the manager flow. Refresh My Reports and
   verify the same deduplicated row becomes **Accepted**.

For at least one combination, report `2 of 8 spools installed; 6 remain; NDT
pending.` Verify confirmation, manager review and My Reports retain that exact
partial statement without displaying an invented completion percentage.
