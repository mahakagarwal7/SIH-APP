# PR: Remove capture stalls and expose background upload state

## Behavior and scope

FIXES step 7.3 removes the work that made capture screens appear frozen. A
camera or library result now enters the thumbnail strip immediately from its
source URI. JPEG resize/compression starts separately, leaves captions and the
rest of the form interactive, and only gates the final durable save or voice
recording until the prepared bytes are available. A failed conversion remains
visible and removable instead of locking the screen.

The text/photo and voice save paths still write app-private evidence before
announcing background work. They do not await reservation, upload,
`finalize_field_media`, processing or submission. The global outbox agent owns
those network phases. My Reports now reads the three local stores directly, so
outbox preparation and SHA-256 hashing cannot block its first render. Queued,
reserving, uploading and finalizing rows use a compact blue **Uploading** chip
with an honest resume message.

This matches the immediate-thumbnail/background-compression behavior in
`docs/UI_SPEC.md` 8.3 and the restrained blue in-progress language in the
supplied mobile references. The full in-app camera viewfinder remains UI step
8.3.

## Contract and decisions

- Existing production `reserve_field_capture`, immutable storage upload,
  `finalize_field_media`, inspection and submission contracts are unchanged.
- Existing RLS and authenticated Supabase client paths are unchanged.
- Photos still use `expo-image-manipulator`: at most 2048 px on the long side
  at 0.82 JPEG quality, with 1600/0.68 and 1280/0.55 bounded retries when output
  exceeds 5 MiB.
- The app records source and prepared byte counts when the picker supplies the
  source size. A desktop proxy using page 1 of `Untitled.pdf` at the equivalent
  2048 px/0.82 settings reduced 4,454,694 bytes to 159,324 bytes, a 96.4%
  reduction. Expo/device codecs and real site photos will vary; the manual
  device check below must record the actual result.
- Voice remains speech-oriented PCM16 WAV: 16 kHz, mono, 16-bit, capped at 25
  seconds (about 800 KiB plus the WAV header). No higher-fidelity capture was
  introduced.
- Backgrounding is non-blocking and durable saved work resumes in the
  foreground. This slice does not claim OS-level uploads while the process is
  suspended.

## Manual device checks

1. On a field account, throttle the connection or enable airplane mode. Save a
   text-only report and stop a voice recording; verify the local saved/review
   state appears promptly and navigation remains available without a network
   response.
2. Pick a full-resolution site photo. Verify its thumbnail appears immediately,
   captions remain editable during **Compressing…**, and the displayed source
   and final sizes update when preparation completes.
3. Record the source size, prepared size and elapsed time for that device photo;
   verify the prepared JPEG is at most 2048 px on its long side and no larger
   than 5 MiB.
4. Start a report sync on a slow connection, open My Reports and verify the
   report row shows the blue **Uploading** chip instead of a blocking screen.
5. Leave the capture screen and background/foreground the app within one second
   after a durable save. Verify the saved row remains, then reconnect and verify
   upload resumes without duplicate reports.
6. Force a photo conversion failure with an unreadable asset if the test device
   permits it. Verify the form remains usable and the failed thumbnail can be
   removed.

## Remaining limitations

- Physical Android timing and real camera reduction have not been measured in
  this automated environment.
- The operating system may suspend JavaScript after backgrounding; saved work
  resumes when Nirmaan returns to the foreground.
- FIXES step 7.4 owns native work-date selection. Steps 7.5 and Phase 8 remain
  separate PRs.
