## Behavior and scope

Roadmap 1.2 replaces the Field Report placeholder with Android voice capture and local draft playback/discard. Recordings stop at 25 seconds and become **Saved on device** only after audio and SQLite metadata persist. Failed writes remain visible and can be retried with the same identifier. Drafts survive logout and are shown only to their creating account.

The screen follows the Nirmaan design book's mobile reporting page: navy/serif heading, report/check/send context, one primary microphone action and precise delivery states. Check/Send, text and photo capture remain explicitly unavailable. Web/iOS show an unavailable voice state.

This PR is stacked on **PR #4, `feature/1.1-my-work`**, which is still unmerged. Its auth/navigation/project dependencies require their own review. No PR merges are included.

## Contract and decisions

The owner approved native WAV evaluation, `com.mahakagarwal.nirmaan`, a local development/debug build, SQLite plus app-private audio, and retention of unsent drafts after logout with same-account access and explicit discard. Release signing and publishing are separate.

The SDK-matched Expo PCM stream feeds a mono PCM16 WAV encoder. The unchanged production worker's byte contract is the boundary: supported actual sample rate, 0.2–25 seconds, exact RIFF length, signal above its silence threshold. See [the recording and persistence contract](../VOICE_CAPTURE.md).

Dependencies: `expo-audio` for microphone PCM and playback; `expo-file-system` for durable app-private audio; `expo-sqlite` for account-scoped metadata; `expo-crypto` for stable draft UUIDs. The local table is not a backend schema change. Existing default-project reads retain authenticated Supabase/RLS behavior. This slice makes no report/media writes or uploads.

## Manual device checks

1. Supply the production publishable/anon key only in the ignored `.env.local`. Build/install the Android debug app using a compatible local JDK/SDK. Sign in with an existing authorized field account and load its project.
2. Open Report, allow microphone access, speak a short site update and stop. Expect **Saved on device. Not sent for review.** Listen to verify intelligible audio. Nothing should appear on the planner website.
3. Record past 25 seconds; expect automatic stop and one draft. Try silence, a very short recording, denied microphone permission, repeated taps and microphone contention; expect explicit errors without a false saved state.
4. With project access already loaded, enable airplane mode and record. Restart the app with a still-valid saved session; the draft must remain available. A cold offline launch cannot load new project access for another capture yet.
5. Start recording, then switch tabs, lock the phone or background the app. Expect the microphone to stop and valid captured audio to save. Check that playback also stops when leaving the screen.
6. Log out and sign into a different account; the first account's local drafts must be absent. Sign back into the original account; its drafts must remain. Discard only after confirming the prompt, then restart and verify both metadata and playback are gone.
7. Test insufficient storage. A stopped recording should offer Retry save and avoid saying saved before success; stay on the screen while retrying. An interrupted write or missing file must be labeled incomplete/missing, with Listen disabled.

## Remaining limitations

Physical-phone audio quality, hardware-rate fallback, headset/call interruption behavior and live production authorization remain separate checks. The local publishable key was still blank when implementation began; the isolated probe uses synthetic identities and does not authenticate to production.

No upload, transcription, confirmation, outbox synchronization, My reports delivery list, status polling, text/photo capture or background recording is included. No Expo Go, iOS recording or release/store readiness claim is made.
