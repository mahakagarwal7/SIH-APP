## Behavior and scope

Manager Schedule was an unavailable placeholder. Roadmap 4.1 adds the active revision banner, a discipline-filtered Gantt-lite activity list, original-baseline toggle, accepted actuals/status, bounded paging and expandable source/plan/actual records. Pending reports remain explicitly separate from the accepted schedule. The structure follows design-book page 3.

## Contract and decisions

- Calls existing `schedule_snapshot(p_project)` through the authenticated Supabase client, then rechecks the same active planner/manager membership and version.
- Parses the production snapshot contract: revision/policy/schedule versions, nodes, all supported disciplines, planned/baseline dates, source metadata, accepted actuals, quantities, percentages and milestone occurrences.
- Rejects field roles before transport and rejects malformed, duplicate, cross-project/revision, reversed-date, invalid-milestone, revoked or version-changed results before display.
- Resets filters, page, baseline toggle and expanded activity when the revision or schedule version changes.
- Uses a common date range for readable phone-width Gantt-lite bars and keeps exact textual dates accessible. Results page locally at 25 activities.
- Adds no dependency, schema, policy, secret, import/activation control or web-backend change. See `docs/SCHEDULE_VIEW.md` for the detailed consistency boundary.

## Manual device checks

Use an owner-designated project with an active imported revision and accepted progress.

1. Open Manager → Schedule and compare revision label/ID, schedule version and activity count with the web Schedule screen.
2. Check all disciplines, then toggle the original baseline. Compare planned, baseline and accepted bars plus their textual dates with web.
3. Open Not started, unresolved progress, In progress, Complete and milestone records. Confirm status and missing actual dates remain exact.
4. Compare hierarchy/WBS, location/stage, calendar, predecessors, baseline, actuals, accepted quantity, percentage/basis and progress date with web.
5. Test more than 25 activities. Confirm stable ranges and no duplicate/missing rows between pages.
6. Activate a designated replacement revision on web, then refresh mobile. Confirm the banner changes and old filter/page/expanded state resets.
7. Load Schedule, disable connectivity and return to the tab. Confirm cached data is labeled stale and refresh is disabled; repeat before any cache exists.
8. Switch projects and confirm the previous revision, activities and controls do not carry over.
9. Revoke/downgrade membership and refresh. Confirm schedule data is unavailable. Confirm a reporter/supervisor cannot open the Manager schedule.

## Remaining limitations

- Live production-RLS, cross-client, emulator and physical-device evidence remain pending designated accounts/projects.
- This slice is read-only. Imports, revision activation and assignments remain web-only.
- Task hierarchy drill-down is roadmap 2.2; this slice validates source nodes but does not add hierarchy navigation.
- The phone view uses a compact common-range track rather than the web's scrollable daily/weekly grid.
