## Behavior and scope

Manager Overview was an unavailable placeholder. Roadmap 6.1 adds exact active
schedule counts, a bounded actionable-claim preview, activity completion by
discipline, six weeks of effective accepted-event counts, and recent accepted
evidence. Every ratio names its numerator and denominator. The screen adds no
overall health score, weighted completion, productivity or schedule forecast.

This PR is stacked on draft PR #17 (`feature/2.2-task-hierarchy`) and reuses the
strict schedule and accepted-history contracts from its dependency chain.
Review and merge its base first.

## Contract and decisions

- Calls existing authenticated `schedule_snapshot(p_project)` and
  `execution_history(p_project)` RPCs plus the existing RLS-protected actionable
  `claims` query. It then rechecks the same active planner/manager membership,
  role and version.
- Counts planned, accepted-finished, accepted-started and unresolved-reported
  activities directly from the active snapshot. Pending claims remain separate
  from accepted activity state.
- Defines discipline bars as finished activity count divided by planned
  activity count in that discipline; both values remain visible.
- Defines the weekly trend as current effective accepted events by recorded work
  date, Monday to Sunday. It is labeled evidence volume, not productivity.
- Shows an exact actionable-claim count and up to five current claim previews,
  with the complete paged queue available through Review.
- Rejects field roles, malformed/cross-project payloads, incomplete claim
  previews, changed/revoked membership and the existing history prototype limit.
- Adds no dependency, schema, policy, RPC, secret, write path or backend change.
  See `docs/MANAGER_OVERVIEW.md` for the complete aggregate boundary.

## Manual device checks

Use owner-designated planner and manager accounts in a project with an active
schedule, actionable claims, corrected accepted evidence and multiple
disciplines.

1. Open Manager → Overview and compare the project, revision and schedule
   version with Manager Schedule and the web overview.
2. Compare planned, accepted-complete and accepted-in-progress counts with the
   exact active snapshot. Confirm reported progress without an accepted start
   appears separately.
3. Compare the actionable claim count with Review. Open each preview and confirm
   its evidence, date, state reason and claim destination; verify the preview is
   capped at five while the exact count remains visible.
4. For each discipline, compare completed/planned counts with Schedule. Confirm
   the bar percentage is only that count ratio and no overall health score is
   shown.
5. Compare the six weekly event counts with current effective History records by
   work date. Confirm superseded events are excluded and corrections use their
   effective replacement.
6. Compare recent accepted activity, kind, evidence, reviewer and acceptance
   time with History, including a corrected record.
7. Test a project with no active schedule, no claims and no accepted history.
   Confirm all three conditions remain explicit rather than blank.
8. Load Overview, disable connectivity and return. Confirm cached counts are
   labeled stale and refresh is disabled; repeat before any cache exists.
9. Switch projects and confirm no prior counts or evidence carry over. Revoke or
   downgrade membership and refresh; confirm the overview becomes unavailable.
10. Sign in as a reporter or supervisor and confirm Manager Overview exposes no
    aggregate or evidence data.

## Remaining limitations

- Live production-RLS, web/mobile parity, emulator and physical-device evidence
  remain pending designated accounts/projects.
- Accepted history keeps the production 20,000-row mobile limit; larger history
  directs the user to a scoped planner export.
- Overall progress, health, cost, schedule variance and productivity remain
  unavailable because no approved weighting or aggregate contract defines them.
